## Two different retentions

**Specimen retention** — how long the physical sample is kept. Days to months,
set by stability and storage capacity. See
[storage](/help/inventory-and-operations/storage-and-custody/).

**Record retention** — how long the *result and its supporting evidence* are
kept. Years. That is this topic.

Confusing them is a common and consequential error. The specimen may be long
disposed of while the record must be kept for a decade.

## The schedule

**Settings → Record retention** holds the minimum period for each class, seeded
from CLIA and editable — a laboratory under a stricter local rule can lengthen
a period without a code change.

| Record class | Default | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Test requisitions | 2 years | CLIA §493.1105(a)(1) |
| Test records and reports | 2 years | §493.1105(a)(2) |
| Quality control records | 2 years | §493.1105(a)(4) |
| Proficiency testing records | 2 years | §493.1105(a)(6) |
| Instrument maintenance | 2 years | §493.1105(a)(5) |
| Personnel and competency | 2 years after employment | §493.1105(a)(7) |
| Immunohaematology | 5 years | §493.1105(a)(3) |
| Cytology slides | 5 years | §493.1105(a)(7)(i) |
| Histology blocks | 2 years | §493.1105(a)(7)(ii) |
| Pathology reports | 10 years | §493.1105(a)(2)(ii) |
| Audit trail | 10 years | 21 CFR Part 11 §11.10(e) |

## These are minimums

Longer may be required by:

- **Local law.** Many jurisdictions exceed CLIA, particularly for paediatric
  records, which are sometimes kept until the patient reaches majority plus a
  period.
- **Litigation.** A limitation period may exceed the retention period. Once
  litigation is anticipated, a **litigation hold** suspends disposal entirely —
  destroying records then is a serious matter regardless of schedule.
- **Accreditation.** CAP and ISO 15189 requirements may exceed CLIA.
- **Clinical value.** A genetic result may be relevant for a lifetime.

## Retention and erasure requests

GDPR gives a right to erasure — but Article 17(3) excludes data retained for a
legal obligation or for public health. Laboratory records held under CLIA fall
within that exclusion while the period runs.

Do not delete records on request without consulting your data protection
officer. "The patient asked" is not, by itself, a lawful basis for destroying a
medical record.

## Disposal

Disposal is itself a controlled act: what was destroyed, when, by whom, by what
method. Record it.

Secure destruction means unrecoverable. For electronic records that means more
than deleting a file; for paper it means cross-cut shredding or incineration,
not a recycling bin.
